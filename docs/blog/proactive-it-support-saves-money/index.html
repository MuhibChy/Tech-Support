Why Proactive IT Support Saves Money

S
Support Manager
• Oct 03, 2026 • ~1 min read
Discover how proactive monitoring and maintenance can reduce your IT costs by up to 40%.
Reactive IT support — waiting for things to break before fixing them — is expensive and disruptive. Proactive IT support flips this model on its head. ## The Cost of Downtime The average cost of IT downtime is $5,600 per minute for businesses. Proactive monitoring can prevent up to 85% of these incidents. ## Benefits of Proactive Support - Reduced downtime and fewer emergencies - Lower long-term maintenance costs - Extended hardware lifespan - Better security posture - Improved employee productivity ## Making the Switch Transitioning to proactive support requires an initial investment in monitoring tools and processes, but the ROI is typically realized within the first 6 months.
